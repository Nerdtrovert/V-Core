INTERPRETATION
- Median cycle count scales approximately linearly with vector size for these workloads.
- At 240 MHz, median CCOUNT measurements correspond to approximately 2.15, 8.15, 32.15, 128.15, and 512.15 us for vector sizes 16, 64, 256, 1024, and 4096 respectively.
- Median cycles per element are approximately 32.19, 30.55, 30.14, 30.03, and 30.01 for those same sizes.
- Larger workloads approach roughly 30 measured cycles per element in this implementation and build. This is an observation for the tested configuration, not a universal ESP32 dot-product cost.
- Wall-clock values are quantized to whole microseconds and include measurement-boundary/overhead effects; they need not convert exactly to CCOUNT-derived times.

UNRESOLVED BASELINE COMPARISON
An earlier Experiment 004 measurement for a 1024-element dot product reported a median of approximately 31,721 cycles (mean approximately 31,737 cycles). Experiment 006 reports 30,755 cycles median (mean 30,771.728 cycles), about 3.05% lower by median.

The repeated Experiment 006 run reproduces its own median exactly, but does not explain the difference from Experiment 004. Do not treat the two measurements as directly equivalent until the source, compiler/build configuration, measurement boundaries, CPU frequency, and runtime conditions have been checked. The cause remains unresolved.
