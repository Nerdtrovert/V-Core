===============================================================
Experiment 003: CPU Frequency Scaling and Wall-Clock Cross-Check
===============================================================

Dot-product benchmarks using 64-element and 1024-element vectors were executed at 160 MHz and 240 MHz on the ESP32. 
Both workloads produced the expected results at both frequencies.

Median cycle counts remained approximately constant across the two configurations, 
while measured wall-clock execution time decreased from 13 µs to 9 µs for the 64-element workload
and from 199 µs to 133 µs for the 1024-element workload.

The large-workload timing reduction of approximately 33.2% closely matched the
theoretical 33.3% reduction expected from increasing CPU frequency by a factor of 1.5. 
This provides a useful cross-check between cycle-count and wall-clock measurements. 
However, the microsecond-resolution timer was insufficient to characterize fine-grained timing variability.
